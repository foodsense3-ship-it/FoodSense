FoodSense — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

FoodSense ("we", "the app") helps you track what you eat. This page explains what information we collect and how it's used.

What we collect

When you sign in, we collect your email address (via Google Sign-In or a one-time email code). Once you're using the app, we collect the information you enter yourself: your profile details (age, height, weight, goals), your meal logs, your food preferences, and app settings.

How we use it

This information is used solely to run the app for you: calculating your calorie targets, tracking your meals, and showing you your own history and insights. We do not sell your data.

Where it's stored

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, a database provider, protected so that only you can access your own data.

Contact

Questions about your data? Email foodsense3@gmail.com.
